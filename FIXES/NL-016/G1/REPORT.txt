2. WHAT A USER CAN ALREADY DO WITH REPD AND DATA CENTRES
All of it sits in the Offtakers layer. That layer is OFF by default and switches on with one checkbox. It was driven
at 1400x900 and at 390x844 (phone, touch).
a) Switch Offtakers on.
   - 1,404 dots appear, with data centres in violet and a status ring.
   - The status line reads "225 from the open pipeline ... 1 skipped".
   - Shot: shots/s2/02-offtakers-desktop-uk.png.
b) Tap a data centre to get the Grid Engine 30/30/30 card.
   - Example: Ampthill Road, Bedford (QuestPit, in planning, IT 720 MW, grid connection 200 MW).
   - It lists 30 substations within 17.7 km, 30 REPD sites within 9.0 km and 30 conventional sites within 81.5 km.
   - REPD within 5/10/20 km: 15/36/139 projects, with 138.13/403.3/2,344.15 MW stated.
   - At 20 km the card reads: operational 451.09 MW, consented 1,179.72 MW, planning 713.34 MW.
   - Nearest 400 kV: Marston Vale, 5.00 km.
   - The REPD tab offers status filters, Draw all and a 92-row CSV.
   - The phone gives the same result.
   - Shots: shots/s3b/03-*.png and 04-*.png.
c) Tap any other offtaker to get its 15 nearest REPD sites and 5 nearest substations.
d) Arrive from Pipeline News (the MAP link), tick Offtakers, then tap the nearby data centre: the REPD project
   appears in the data centre's card.
   - Tested on REPD 9077 (Green Lane, Stewartby, 30 MW solar).
   - It is item 9, at 2.76 km. The convergence file on main gives dc1_km 2.761, so the two agree.
   - Shots: shots/s4b/05b-*-dccard.png.
GridAtlas computes no "shared connection": distances are straight lines, and there are no connection references to
join on.

3. URLS THAT OPEN GRIDATLAS WITHOUT NEW GRIDATLAS CODE
- REPD project, using the Pipeline News MAP contract (CONTRACT_PARAMS in atlas-receiver-v9-7.js):
  /gridatlas/atlas/?repd_ref=9077&technology=solar&latitude=52.0702499&longitude=-0.5144965&zoom=12
  - Centres on the project.
  - Ticks 400/275/132 kV, Subs and Solar.
  - Nearest 400 kV is Marston Vale at 2.26 km.
  - The identity check fails locally because parquet is blocked. Live behaviour is UNVERIFIED.
- REPD by reference only: ?repd_ref=9668&technology=bess
  Fails locally (parquet). Pipeline News code says this was measured working live on REPD 13429. UNVERIFIED.
- A data centre:
  ?latitude=52.09&longitude=-0.49&zoom=13&project=Data+Centre+Campus%2C+Ampthill+Road%2C+Bedford
  - WORKS: the camera lands on the site, the card is titled with the name, and nearest 400 kV is measured
    (Marston Vale, 5.00 km).
  - It is the generic arrival card: no data-centre fields and no pin.
  - Shot: shots/s4/07-E3b-*.png.
- No URL parameter switches Offtakers on or opens a data-centre card. &offtakers=1 was tried and does nothing.
