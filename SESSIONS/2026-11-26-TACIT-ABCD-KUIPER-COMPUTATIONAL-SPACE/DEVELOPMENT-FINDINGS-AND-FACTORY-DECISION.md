26 November 2026 — decision recorded following the owner's instruction to retain the factory within VEDIC.

## Decision
Keep the existing Package Factory in Ventusltd/vedic-. Do not create a separate package-factory repository, duplicate the factory, migrate its implementation or divert TACIT B from the current turns 8–10. Kuiper remains the computational environment; VEDIC is a semantic instrument, currently also hosting the factory. The owner has confirmed this organisational decision; it is not a grant of package admission, identity allocation, redistribution or publication authority.

A separate repository is a possible future architectural decision, not a commitment. Reconsider only when there are at least three independently verified and internally admitted software families, a stable versioned package specification and typed execution interface, reproducible builds and established licensing/distribution controls. If justified by real cross-estate consumers, extract the existing implementation and preserve its history, contracts, artifact hashes, tests and rollback. Do not rewrite it. Obtain owner agreement before separation. These are proposed readiness criteria, not new factory admission gates.

## Kuiper computational space
The refreshed register reports 97,540,883,985 issued historical line-occurrence keys, 116,449,247,985 total address positions, 16,774 commits and 101 repositories. These are not unique executable functions, physical transistors or a measure of operations per second. Preserve the mathematical placement law and exact integer keys. Properties and selection are sparse, versioned overlays on source identity; CPU/GPU execution remains a separate runtime operation. No GPU speedup has been demonstrated.
