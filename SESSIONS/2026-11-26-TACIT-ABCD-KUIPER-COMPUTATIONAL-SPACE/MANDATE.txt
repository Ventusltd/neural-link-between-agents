1. THE CORE MISSION

GlobalGrid2050 is an energy-infrastructure computational estate, not simply a collection of applications. Our next objective is to develop Kuiper into a programmable computational environment in which existing mathematical and engineering intelligence can be found, classified, connected, executed and independently tested. VEDIC is one instrument operating within that environment; it translates supported human intentions into typed selections and candidate computational patterns. It does not replace Kuiper, the Periodic Table, Modular Star, Spider, the Package Factory or the verification estate.

The next three turns must advance operational relationships rather than merely describe them. The eight already verified computational addresses, thirty-two properties and five conditional bonds are the common research cohort. Do not start a larger census merely to increase the numbers. First make these existing capabilities work together with traceable selection, actual execution, proper refusal, independent verification and reversible visual representation. Once the process is reliable, the same architecture can be extended without replacing the underlying identities.

Kuiper's historical address space is not physical processing hardware. A deterministic coordinate is an address of a source occurrence, not an executable transistor or a promise of computation. The actual CPU or GPU executes authorised software through an appropriate runtime. The potential is to reduce discovery, composition and validation costs by selecting relevant sparse address sets and their verified relationships rather than scanning the entire estate. Measure this potential experimentally.

Four active workstreams are now authorised for this cycle: TACIT A, source and identity; TACIT B, package and runtime; TACIT C, independent verification; TACIT D, Kuiper computational selection and interaction. All other TACIT work is historical producer evidence. Do not reactivate earlier agents, create competing agents or use their silence as a blocker. The supervisor remains the coordination writer, not a fifth implementation agent.
