## Candidate contracts

Research labels LEI-01 through LEI-08 are local identifiers only, not permanent STARS identities or allocated VEDIC numbers. All VEDAS below are 1–5 words and must be checked against the current vocabulary before formal admission.

### LEI-01 — Statutory intelligence / CHECK STATUTORY RIGHTS
Kernel: a dated, jurisdiction-aware rule evaluation with explicit applicability and exceptions.
Inputs: as-of date, jurisdiction, actor type and evidenced authority, work purpose, land relationship, impact duration, notice/consent evidence, reviewed rule-pack identity.
Output: applicable requirements, satisfied/unsatisfied/unknown predicates, evidence locators, review requirement. Never a grant of permission.
Proof target: distinguish licence-holder versus other actor, host land versus non-host land, temporary versus permanent effect, existing versus new infrastructure. Missing facts and proposed-only rules cannot establish current rights.

### LEI-02 — Regulatory counterfactual / COMPARE LEGISLATION
Kernel: compare two explicitly identified rule versions without conflating legal status and hypothetical scenarios.
Inputs: baseline and scenario rule-pack refs, dates, jurisdiction, identical fictional case and facts.
Output: changed predicates, unchanged conditions, unresolved exceptions, separate baseline/scenario evidence. 
Proof target: an uncommenced proposal cannot alter the baseline; unrelated rules remain unchanged; missing date or version refuses. Reuse LEI-01.

### LEI-03 — Economic appraisal / ASSESS ECONOMIC IMPACT
Kernel: transparent discounted comparison of costs and benefits across time.
Inputs: baseline and option annual cashflow or social-value series, base year, real/nominal currency basis, explicitly sourced discount schedule, horizon, stakeholder scope, uncertainty and missing-value flags.
Output: separate present values, net difference, sensitivity and excluded/unquantified impacts. Do not confuse social value with private return or use an assessment total as a project forecast.
Proof target: a synthetic two-period known-answer calculation, unit/basis mismatch refusal, undiscounted year zero, missing value remains unquantified, no double counting. Confirm IA6 economic parameters from the source before any replication; none are hardcoded here.

### LEI-04 — Evidence sufficiency / IDENTIFY MISSING EVIDENCE
Kernel: determine the smallest sufficient set of facts needed for a declared bounded question.
Inputs: question contract, evidence IDs, timestamps, completeness states and required fields.
Output: supported, contradicted or unknown predicates; unresolved evidence requests and stop condition. Missing, stale, unsearched, withheld and zero are distinct.
Proof target: unknown is never coerced into permission, zero or verified success; no unnecessary reads after evidence suffices. Reuse the existing SEED bounded discovery/retrieval and refusal patterns where contracts fit.

### LEI-05 — Constrained graph / MODEL CONSTRAINED GRAPH
Kernel: represent civil infrastructure feasibility as typed relationships and independent constraints.
Inputs: small synthetic/authorised abstract graph of asset classes and edges, explicit connectivity, units, limits, permissions and uncertainty.
Output: typed graph and constraint ledger suitable for reviewed screening; no real-world access routes, site selection or security-sensitive operational outputs.
Proof target: spatial proximity is not connectivity; legal rights are not physical capacity; unknown edges stay unknown; no fabricated capacity or permission. Reuse existing GridAtlas geometry/identity and Ventus Grid Engine proofs only when appropriate, with declared electrical assumptions.

### LEI-06 — Bottleneck discovery / IDENTIFY LIMITING CONSTRAINT
Kernel: isolate the predicate or set of predicates limiting a stated abstract feasibility objective, without repeatedly analysing all evidence.
Inputs: LEI-05 graph, objective and ordered constraint evaluations.
Output: witnessed limiting constraints, ties, insufficient-evidence state and next targeted investigation.
Proof target: two independent blockers must not collapse into one; missing data cannot be ranked as failure or clearance; removal of one blocker does not prove feasibility. Reuse LEI-04 and LEI-05.

### LEI-07 — System counterfactual / COMPARE FEASIBILITY SCENARIOS
Kernel: compare controlled alternative assumptions with the baseline, preserving scope and dependency evidence.
Inputs: identical fictional network, baseline, declared changed constraints, scenario version and appropriate model assumptions.
Output: changed and unchanged screening results, sensitivity and limits; no inferred real connection entitlement.
Proof target: unchanged inputs yield identical results, a hypothetical change affects only declared dependencies, conservation checks where an electrical model is used. Reuse LEI-02, LEI-05 and LEI-06.

### LEI-08 — Higher-order feasibility / ASSESS INFRASTRUCTURE FEASIBILITY
Kernel: combine evidence, legal applicability, graph constraints and economic comparison into a transparent civil feasibility screening instrument.
Inputs: fictional or appropriately authorised aggregate scenario, evidence refs, approved rule packs, declared engineering model, cost and benefit assumptions.
Output: feasible under stated screening assumptions, infeasible under stated assumptions, or insufficient evidence; independent legal/technical/economic ledgers, review requirements, resource receipt. Never a planning approval, land right, capacity allocation or operational instruction.
Proof target: known-answer synthetic scenario, refusal for any absent mandatory evidence, explicit scope and lawful review, no override of a failed legal/engineering gate by favourable cost. Reuse LEI-01 through LEI-07 as applicable.
