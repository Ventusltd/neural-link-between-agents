## 4. Definition of kernel, SEED and instrument

The owner asked to define the intellectual kernels underlying the 477k lines rather than count every leaf. An intellectual kernel is a transferable computational principle underlying a valuable achievement. A SEED is its recoverable executable embodiment: exact source identities, inputs, outputs, dependencies, evidence and limits. An instrument exposes that capability for use. A verified composition may itself become a higher-order SEED. These are project definitions, not claims of mathematical irreducibility or a new operating-system kernel.

Seven-step build order: start from a remarkable outcome, find its mechanism, preserve exact identity and dependency closure, verify a sufficient assembly, abstract a transferable principle, apply it to a different task, and compose verified capabilities into higher-order instruments. The objective is not necessarily shortest source text, but smallest sufficient work with preserved evidence.
