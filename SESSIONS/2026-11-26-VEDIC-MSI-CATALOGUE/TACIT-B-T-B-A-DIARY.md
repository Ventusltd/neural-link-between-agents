## What I learned today
The owner's original ambition was clearer than our initial implementation. VEDIC is not a new programming language or a dictionary of disconnected verbs such as ORIENT, MEASURE and REPRESENT. It is intended as a detailed, continuous, natural English digital twin of actual software, understandable to people who have never learned to code. The ultimate experiment is whether a person assisted by a PC and AI can reconstruct functioning software from the English alone. A summary, a word list, or a database of source rows is not a substitute for that representation. Prose must preserve execution sequences, dependencies, state, mathematical rules, exact units/constants, exceptions, visual behavior, errors and limitations. Source-linked storage is a means of retaining evidence, not the primary reading experience.

Earlier TACIT work on canonical computational identities, dependencies, authenticated execution, package factory, deterministic mathematics, editor/runtime integration, regressions and performance is valuable infrastructure. It remains reusable, but six agents independently expanding architecture risked obscuring the owner objective. T16 combined evidence included 97 passes and three open conditions in independent witness; T17 mixed-origin shorthand ambiguity remained a known regression and signed browser acceptance was not complete at the earlier checkpoint. Do not promote historical work based on component tests alone. Preserve original branches, PRs and evidence.
