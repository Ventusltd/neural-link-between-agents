# SEED computational anatomy — research report and cross-agent handover

Date: 27 November 2026. Status: research findings recorded; next work proposed, no production release. Role: SEED research synthesis (self-declared). This document is not an agent activation, permission expansion or proof of independent acceptance.

## Why this work exists

The estate has accumulated nearly half a million byte-distinct lines through AI-assisted development. The human bottleneck was invention, prioritisation, verification and integration rather than typing code. The objective is to inherit intellectual discoveries without repeating the original cognitive cost. We want to understand the forest through what its seeds can grow, not memorise the DNA.

The five objectives are understanding through 100 analytical lenses, capability distillation, operational relevance, recursive composition and reduced cognitive load. Play and exploration are valuable when they yield real applications. Retain a simple 2D browser environment first; defer a separate 3D IDE unless evidence shows it improves learning. Gephi and Spider can reveal relationships but do not prove a working instrument.

## GridAtlas: the reference achievement

The owner demonstrated railway layers from national scale to local alignments and satellite context, and a lightning cell with 311 optical observations leading to five nearest substations and ten nearby renewable sites (13 grouped projects). The displayed archive reports 370,908 flashes, 340 of 366 days searched, 115 partial days, 26 unsearched days, and a stale collector. It distinguishes unknown from zero and proximity from evidence of physical impact. The layer catalogue extends across electricity, transport, industry, communications, energy development and more; unloaded layers are not proven by their labels.

A major research insight is the selective loading architecture. MapLibre and public data sources supply important foundations, but the integration determines when and how data becomes useful. The inspected GridAtlas release records pre-snapped topology, compressed Parquet and render-ready GeoJSON, content-addressed caching and direct worker loading of the 400 kV layer (4,106 features, 1,469,778 bytes). The later composition describes streaming, deferred retrieval and payload lifetime management. These mechanisms may transfer to other large-information applications. Establish their causal contribution through bounded tests rather than assuming all load is crash-free.

Correction: the 1,777-page, 777,360-word print is generated by GridAtlas FILE > Print source code for AI review, not Spider Printer. The owner reports larger, sometimes difficult-to-load exports above 3,000 pages with different loaded layers. Spider Printer is a repository reference graph, not the source printer. Export size is neither unique code count nor proof of operational necessity.

## Proposed independent research lanes (owner assignment required)

### A — Operational census
Read the permanent STARS schema and use read-only snapshots. Map every indexed line to products and shared infrastructure. Separate historical, current scan, published dependency closure, exercised source and demonstrably essential source. Include unknown/unmapped populations, overlapping membership and mutually exclusive estate totals. Do not use line-ID maxima as counts. Produce auditable receipts and a compact per-product HTML table.

### B — Functional anatomy and reproducible seeds
Investigate the highest-coverage 100 recurring assemblies, backwards from actual outputs. Define contracts, replay known-answer and refusal cases, locate complete code/data/dependency closure and identify transferable mechanisms. Record bounded proof versus inference. Use no speculative composition as a verified capability. Keep existing software and permanent IDs intact. Deliver a simple interactive browser catalogue with actual demonstrations or evidence links.

### C — Selective loading and resilience
Trace GridAtlas's critical and deferred data paths, precomputation, workers, compressed partitions, caching and source export. Establish cold/warm measurements and failure injection tests. Use the CVAA study as a proposed contract; implement validated checks only in an assigned test branch. Distinguish application, dependency and harness failures. No live release edits, no untested vaccines, no simultaneous writes to another agent's scope.

## Coordination and completion

Read AGENTS.md and PROTOCOL.md on invocation. Confirm exact snapshot, scope, head and ownership; acknowledge through the canonical issue bus when an issue is assigned.
