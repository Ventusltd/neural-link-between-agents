27 November 2026 | Owner-requested study and corrective implementation | Coordination only in Neural Link; product changes require destination-specific authority.

## Purpose and evidence baseline

Neural Link has become the durable intellectual memory of the Ventus estate, but its weakest property is the reliability of its *current* instructions. A durable record is not necessarily a correct, current, delivered or authorised record. The aim of this turn is to make restarting, coordinating and validating work measurably safer without creating another platform, duplicating producers or increasing the volume of prose. The three agents each receive approximately one third of the engineering effort, including study, implementation, negative tests and cross-review. Names NEURAL A, NEURAL B and NEURAL C are proposed routing labels, not authenticated identities or evidence that sessions are running. Existing TACIT agents may claim a lane only after checking for conflicts and acknowledging their current obligations.

Distinguish transport failure, evidence failure and authority failure. GitHub comments are editable and deletable; an append-only convention is not immutability. ## Common delivery and acceptance

All three agents start by reading the entire mandate, refreshing source heads, claiming a single lane and posting an ACK in the coordination issue. Reserve distinct branches and paths; propose interfaces before editing. Record current PR overlap and cooperate with existing owners. Work concurrently only when sessions actually run; no fictitious activity, fixed-duration guarantees or sleep loops. First turn: baseline and threat/failure map. Second: implement scoped fixes and fixtures. Third: independently review and reconcile. A reviews C, B reviews A, C reviews B. A review binds the exact commit and test receipt; any subsequent change requires renewed review. Each agent contributes approximately equal effort and reports measured work, not inflated token counts.

Deliver a concise findings register, exact source and output SHAs, reproducible commands, positive and negative results, unresolved limitations, dependency/ownership handoffs and a rollback plan. Use a draft PR per lane. C coordinates integration with existing PR owners after cross-review; no agent merges another's branch, changes protected settings or moves production pointers without applicable approval. If checks are unavailable, preserve the blocked status and stop that promotion. Prefer a small set of durable, deterministic fixes over more documentation or architectural expansion. Final acceptance requires successful exact-head tests, independent review, owner-policy compliance, no known regressions on sampled healthy paths, and a separately verified readback wherever publication is authorised.

The objective is not to prove Neural Link cannot fail. It is to reduce recurrence, detect failures early, recover work without session memory, and ensure evidence and authority survive the handover. Record remaining risks honestly.
