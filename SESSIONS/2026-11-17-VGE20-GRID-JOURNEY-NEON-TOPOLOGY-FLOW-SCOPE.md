# Ventus Grid Engine 2.0 — Grid Journey / Neon Train over real GridAtlas
**17 November 2026 | Technical design study / source audit / minimal pilot | Not a released feature**

## Goal and owner instruction
Create a minimalist **neon train journey through real GIS**. The user taps a mapped data centre, offtaker, renewable project or substation; the existing **GridAtlas Vicinity 30/30/30** is the initial discovery pool; the camera remains inside **the existing GridAtlas MapLibre map**; a small moving neon pulse traverses **only documented electrical connectivity** when a source-supported connection can be traced. Where none is documented, offer a clearly separate **Explore nearby mapped assets** mode, not a fictional wire. Branches, transformers, parallel circuits, cycles and dead ends are visited in deterministic logical order. A student must learn *where we can trace*, *where the evidence stops*, and *what the published time-specific flow measurements say*, without a load-flow or fault-current simulation.

The pulse is a **visual cursor, not a physical charge packet nor a representation of real-time AC electron flow**. A cable / substation graph is not necessarily a GIS trace: network topology, asset identity and line geometry each have their own evidence and coordinates.

## Audited existing code: DO NOT REBUILD
- **Live atlas renderer/composer**: `Ventusltd/gridatlas/atlas/current.json` resolves modular cartridges including sld-sandbox, substation-intelligence, uk-lightning-overlay, uk-offtakers-overlay. MapLibre is the authoritative base; add a separately declared, removable journey overlay **after the existing cartridges**, not a new standalone web app, not React, not a replacement GIS shell. Use lifecycle/manifest hash checks and clean add/remove source/layer handling.
- **Vicinity 90**: `gridatlas/tools/offtakers/src/06b-dc-engine.js`, `docs/OFFTAKERS-SOURCE.md`. Data-centre tap works with up to 30 nearest ≥33 kV substations, up to 30 relevant REPD projects within 40 km, and 30 nearest conventional generation assets (regardless of distance, displayed links limited to 100 km). The actual pool may contain fewer valid records; do **not** hard-code exactly 90 connected nodes. This is a *spatial candidate pool*, not a circuit graph. Reuse stable exact IDs, existing geodesy, grouping, UI chips and existing neon MapLibre line style. The original `nearestSubstations()` shows five; the 30-substation set uses `gridScope.scope`. Their counts, radius conditions and fail-closed unknowns must be preserved.
- **Transmission topology**: `Ventusltd/data-grid-gb/derived/gb-transmission-network.v1.json` (source ETYS 2025), described in `data-grid-gb/README.md`: 921 sites, 2,679 nodes, 1,392 circuits, 1,472 transformers and 2,230 *planned* changes. Source-derived transmission asset graph, **not a GIS cable geometry or power flow solution**. Separate `connection-points.v3.json` has 886 ≥132 kV sites, **489 mapped/joined coordinates and 397 without**; identity matches remain qualified. Not every Vicinity 33/66/132 kV OSM site can be mapped to ETYS. Unknown coordinates remain unlocated, not discarded or assigned to the nearest apparent match.
- **Existing graph code**: `Ventusltd/ventus-grid-engine/engine/network-topology.js` indexes ETYS sites/nodes, circuit and transformer edges and excludes future `planned_change` rows from operative connectivity. `engine/electrical-distance.js` implements `between(index,from,to,{maxHops,voltageKv})` with breadth-first fewest-hop paths and `within(index,key,{hops,voltageKv})`, refusing illegal voltage jumps except across an explicit transformer. Reuse directly with pinned source and its tests. **This BFS is a single shortest-hop traversal, NOT an all-branches rail itinerary.** New edge-coverage planner must be isolated and must never alter this existing contract.
- **Journey / route graphics already exist**: `Ventusltd/globalgrid2050/energy-transition-simulator/202610290235/mod/engine/journey.mjs` implements `go substation 132` / `next substation`, camera position and actual substation index. `kuiper-grid/drawing/whole-site-routes.js` and `whole-site-route-view.js` implement ID-based route geometry and native canvas tracing for provisional feeder circuits; `kuiper-belt/see-through` visualises travel to ~5,800 substations; Kuiper `cosmos/wafer.tsv`, `cosmos/pulse.tsv` and edition modules provide visual animation patterns. **Reuse graphics/transition ideas only**, respecting each application's provenance; a synthetic whole-site feeder route does not prove a real GB transmission connection. Search before asserting any separate Faraday Pulse physics module is a live/certified circuit solver.
- **Old corridor caveat**: `data-grid-gb/docs/routing/{LEGACY_LAYER_AUDIT,METHOD_AND_LIMITATIONS}.md`: existing railway/road line data are display-only and lack graph-critical node identity/grade separation; a railway or roadway physical crossing is not an electrical junction. Cable corridor screening is a separate engineered problem; it cannot be silently promoted as a cable route. The proposed journey is simpler: **browse an already documented network**, do not infer a new underground cable route.
- **Lightning**: `Ventusltd/lightning` contains seven-day optical flash feed plus archived day shards and coverage semantics; `gridatlas/docs/LIGHTNING-CARTRIDGE.md`, `tools/lightning/src/` describe a separate overlay with a source-built module. Reuse the *time-window/MapLibre overlay* pattern as an optional weather layer. EUMETSAT optical flashes include in-cloud detections and are **not verified ground strikes**. Never imply a recorded flash damaged a circuit or prompted an outage.

## Published power direction evidence and what it really supports
1. **NESO ETYS 2025**: https://www.neso.energy/publications/electricity-ten-year-statement-etys/etys-documents-and-appendices . Appendix **A** has physical system diagrams, Appendix **B** connectivity/network parameters, Appendix **C** future/present **modelled power-flow scenario diagrams**, published 30 June 2026. Note scenario/season/year and diagram units, geographically schematic nature and uncertainty. **Do not label modelled winter peak as a present real-time MW flow**. Source Appendix A/C could be manually converted to a bounded illustrated subgraph with exact reference/panel citation *only after review*; line GIS shape still must come from independently justified geographic geometry.
2. **NESO Day Ahead Constraint Flows and Limits**: https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/day-ahead-constraint-flows-and-limits and field documentation https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/day-ahead-constraint-flows-and-limits/day_ahead_constraint_flows_and_limits . Current public CSV + Scotland and England/Wales diagrams (rights: NESO Open Data Licence). Half-hour boundary **forecast** `Constraint Group`, time, `Flow_MW`, `Limit_MW`, with **diagram arrows defining the positive direction**. A negative value reverses that defined boundary orientation **only if the sign interpretation/diagram revision is validated**. Boundary flow is the **aggregate across a defined boundary**, not necessarily a measurement on an individual circuit. Snapshot forecast is from day-ahead scheduled state, not intraday metering. Do not animate all individual lines in the region in the boundary's direction merely because that boundary is net exporting.
3. **NESO congestion/limits**: https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/operational-transparency-forum-network-congestion-data/network_congestion_forecast_and_actual_data . Useful *constraint-limit* context; do not conflate actual/forecast **constraint limits** with actual per-circuit flow or available connection capacity.
4. **Elexon Insights/BMRS**: https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/api-documentation/endpoint (public APIs) and https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/interconnector-flows . Useful series include generation outturn (per type; per BM Unit where available), demand, physical notifications, and **historical interconnector imports/exports** (`/generation/outturn/interconnectors`). These can supply **time context**, actual interconnector direction and source/consumer screening, **NOT the unmetered flow direction on every local circuit**. Generation by fuel type can exclude embedded generation; avoid equating BMU metering with total distribution injection. Preserve measurement timestamps, settlement periods, raw sign conventions, revised values, source coverage, access policy and rights. Cite source-specific endpoints and decoded sign policy in code/tests, never guess.
5. **UK Power Networks SGT real-time/historical measurements**: https://dso.ukpowernetworks.co.uk/dso-data and https://ukpowernetworks.opendatasoft.com/explore/assets/ukpn-super-grid-transformer-header/ . Latest available half-hour SGT/GSP voltages, current, P and Q are useful **at the transmission/distribution interface**. Publisher states sampled GSP coverage ~1/5, half-hour refresh and CC BY 4.0, with registration/login indicated on a dataset page: test unauthenticated access rather than assuming it. For some **primary transformer** datasets the published documentation warns that polarity/directionality is inconsistent; use a qualified from→to reference only when source-specific convention has been checked. See https://open-data.org.uk/dataset/ukpowernetworks/ukpn-primary-transformer-power-flow-historic-half-hourly-spn . Refuse to infer power direction from positive current magnitude.
6. **Lightning overlays** are observational environmental context, not input to flow direction, routing permission or circuit status.

### Hard rule: three independent meanings of 'direction'
- **Journey direction:** order the neon marker moves from stop A to B to C. Purely a chosen learning itinerary; does not show electrical flow.
- **Circuit connectivity:** a source-supported physical circuit or transformer relates nodes A↔B; an ordinary AC circuit graph is navigable in either direction even though real-time flow can reverse.
- **Power-flow direction:** sign of a **time-stamped, explicitly defined** measured or published forecast/scenario datum over a named circuit, transformer or boundary. Its time, basis, spatial granularity and sign frame must be visible. When data only exists for a boundary, render a **boundary arrow/band** independent from the circuit pulse, never assign branch MW by interpolation. Without enough data show 'Flow direction unavailable for this segment.'

## Minimal architecture

### Stage 0: separate identities and source types
Keep `asset_id`, `namespace` (ETYS site/node, OSM substation, REPD project, DC lead, conventional station), source and version. Maintain a reviewed crosswalk `source_identity -> GIS feature ID` with exact verified matches, location provenance and ambiguity outcomes. Geometry cannot substitute for identity.

`discoveryPool = vicinity(origin, {subs:30,repd:30,conventional:30,radiusKm:40})` is an illustrative *adapter contract* for the existing engine, not an assertion that all three sets use the same radius or that it is an existing callable function.

`topologyGraph = networkTopology.index(pinnedEtysB).graph()` from original owner module. Build a **bounded induced subgraph from a physically connected seed**, not a graph by joining the 90 closest dots. Include at most the requested geographic radius + justified boundary-stub nodes; unknown-location nodes may exist in topology but cannot be silently plotted.

### Stage 1: deterministic 'all routes available' edge-coverage
Literal enumeration of *all simple paths* in a cyclic electrical graph is combinatorial/exponential. The correct user-visible promise is **visit every eligible, source-backed edge within the selected subgraph**, with coverage statistics; not every combinatorial possible journey.

- Restrict candidates by selected source, radius, voltage and status, pin the full node/edge list. Give separate lanes: real `circuit`, real `transformer`, `not_operational_yet` future planned change (never traversed as live), `gis_only`/unknown edges (not traversed in electrical mode).
- Determine connected components; start at exact origin site if supported. Treat circuit edges as undirected **for graph navigation** (do not confuse this with power flow direction).
- Sort deterministic adjacency by (higher voltage first, equipment kind, operator/source grade, stable circuit ID and far-node ID); this is an exploration order, **not a claim of electrical hierarchy or measured flow**. A voltage transition can be crossed only by an explicit transformer in the graph.
- Walk a depth-first **edge-covering traversal**; mark visited **edge IDs**, not just vertices; backtrack along already-traversed segments to reach unvisited branches, with a subtle return trail. Visit parallel circuits separately when IDs distinguish them. For cyclic graphs, traversing each edge once where possible is acceptable; if complete on-foot/train-style continuous traversal cannot occur without repeated edges, repeat explicitly instead of teleporting. Cap hops, segments, time, off-screen moves and number of animated objects. Compute coverage fraction: eligible graph edges visited / eligible graph edges total; list disconnected/unlocated/unlicensed/missing-geometry segments separately.
- An optional `Next branch` selects deterministic alternate branch. An optional `Shortest published path` uses the existing `electrical-distance.between`; a `Visit all` journey uses new edge-cover plan. Offer a small list of *K* alternate site-to-site paths only if the graph and compute budget support it; never promise mathematically every possible simple path.
- **Geometry support states**: `mapped_polyline` (source-supported exact circuit route with GIS line ID) → animate a thin neon pulse along actual line; `topology_only` (published circuit endpoints but physical route not proven) → show a distinct dotted **schematic connector between located endpoints**, optionally navigate without a line-shaped trace, clearly labelled not a mapped cable; `unlocated` → list node in itinerary but show **no invented line** or off-map line. `vicinity_only` → Exploratory mode, optional faint proximity ray explicitly 'not a circuit'.
- On source update, recompute source bundle hash and route digest; never mutate an immutable release or silently reuse a mismatched cached itinerary.

### Stage 2: minimally visible native GIS presentation
One extra **Journey** action in the existing substation/data-centre card. One narrow bottom card with: `Explore | Published circuits | Flow context`, selected radius, filter, source date, `Start / Next / Reset`, covered edges and the current 'stop'. No faux train, no separate map, no big timetable, no green/red 'connection capacity' indicator.

Render **one active neon moving dot and a thin highlighted line with glow**, station dots for visited stops; keep all existing GIS layers, zoom/pan, lightning and source cards. Animate at a constant **screen presentation speed**, never tie speed to measured MW or imply speed of electricity. Only orient an extra **flow-arrow indicator** when true temporal flow sign is available for *that matching asset scale*. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: manual step mode and static highlighted path; keyboard and 44 px hit targets. If a continually moving trace auto-starts, provide an accessible pause/stop mechanism. Do not borrow ticker 'no pause' decision for this separate playback control.

### Stage 3: optional context modules, not part of MVP
- **NESO boundary-flow overlay**: labelled signed MW/limits for selected date/settlement interval, boundary polygons/diagram crosswalk when sourced. Arrows for boundary sign only, no universal claim that all cables point the same way. No physical distribution inference.
- **Elexon BMRS overlay**: GB mix, BMU production and interconnector import/export alongside time slider; physical graph remains the same, metadata clock separate.
- **UKPN measured GSP/SGT overlay** only if stable ID mapping and sign convention confirmed; coverage count and restricted data access reported.
- **Kuiper / Faraday / SLD**: optional future handoff to separate pedagogical model, using verified route/asset IDs but NEVER treating illustrative SLD simulation as telemetry or actual site loading.
- **Lightning**: fade/date-filtered public optical flashes along the explored geographic region, show source coverage and unknown periods; do not present flash proximity as a failure/strike diagnosis.

## Suggested interface/graph contracts
- `network_nodes: [{id,source_id,site_id,voltage_kv,geom_ref,located,identity_basis}]`
- `network_edges: [{id,kind:'circuit'|'transformer',from,to,source_ref,source_date,operational_state,geometry_status,geometry_ref,voltage_kv,source_bundle_hash}]`
- `journey: {mode:'published_edge_cover',origin,region,radius_km,source_bundle_sha,sort_profile:'voltage-source-id-v1',steps:[{edge_id,from,to,repeated,geometry_status}],counts:{eligible,visited,revisited,unlocated,excluded},limits:[...]}`
- `flow_observation: {spatial_basis:'boundary'|'interconnector'|'SGT'|'circuit'|'BMU',reference_id,time_utc,value_mw,value_kind:'measured'|'forecast'|'scenario',positive_direction_basis,source_url,source_snapshot_sha,licence,sign_reviewed,associated_edge_id:null}`. **`associated_edge_id` MUST remain null unless individual circuit identity is independently justified**.

## Propose three incremental implementation gates
**G0 — audit and prototype (offline only):** isolate the original exact modules and data; map ETYS 921-site topology against 489 located v3 sites and live GridAtlas map assets, counting exact/ambiguous/unknown matches; make a synthetic 9-node branching/parallel/transformer/cycle fixture and a second fixture from real pinned Appendix B IDs; test edge-cover traversal on both. Fetch samples of NESO boundary CSV, Elexon relevant endpoints and UKPN SGT dataset, review sign definitions/rights; produce no fabricated direction arrows.

**G1 — optional preview-only GridAtlas cartridge:** origin and regional radius from existing 30/30/30, deterministic edge-cover route for genuinely matched transmission assets, static/source-traceable schematic fallback for topology-only, one moving dot, source-aware visited counts, no current/power-flow simulation; checked isolated MapLibre composition. Retain the real GIS and all native map controls. Do not modify live `atlas/current.json`.

**G2 — direction data adapters:** NESO half-hour boundary forecasts & ETYS Appendix C scenario, Elexon interconnectors and system context, UKPN SGT if qualified; update *metadata overlay* only, not topology/drawn route identity. Source/sign/time provenance and coverage tests required before any 'direction of power flow' claim. Independent reviewer audits a sample of reversed signs, boundary vs circuit confusion, present vs future and no-data cases.

### Negative tests that must fail closed
1. Two substations 0.2 km apart with **no published circuit** → no electrical journey edge.
2. Same visible GIS line crossing another without a named node/transformer → do not junction.
3. Published circuit with no GIS geometry → do not depict as exact physical line.
4. Two voltage bands without explicit transformer → no cross-voltage traverse.
5. Planned 2029 reinforcement → not part of 2026 live path.
6. Repeated circuit listed at both ends → one stable edge, not duplicate.
7. Two parallel circuits → distinct edge visits.
8. Loop and branch → deterministic complete eligible-edge coverage, explicit returns, no infinite recursion.
9. ETYS forecast winter peak and NESO day-ahead sign → not marked current metered circuit flow.
10. Elexon generator injection, interconnector imports and UKPN GSP measurement → no unverified same-circuit flow assignment.
11. UKPN primary transformer inconsistent sign → render unknown direction until validated.
12. Lightning optical flash near a journey line → do not imply ground strike/fault/outage.
13. Unlocated sites/ambiguous OSM name → no automatic lat/lon or fictitious connection.
14. Receiver fail/hidden card/phone rotation → show retriable nonblocking state, don't freeze GridAtlas.
15. Circle includes 90 places but only 12 source-backed circuit edges → say 12 eligible, not '90 routes'.

## Outcome
This is a **good, feasible standalone learning enhancement built from existing code**, provided it advertises *electrical topology*, *cartographic geometry*, *visual journey order* and *power-flow direction* as four different things. **Do not add a full AC solver or physics model as a prerequisite.** First demonstrate a traced branch, an honest stop where evidence ends, and a time-stamped NESO boundary arrow in a small native GridAtlas preview.

No source code, historical release, active GridAtlas manifest, homepage pointer or scheduled workflow was changed by writing this scope.
